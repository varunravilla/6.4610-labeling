Guideline
  • Relevant = about cross-border trade (tariffs, duties, quotas, deals, import/export limits, deficits) or a policy or concrete claim that would change costs, prices, production, jobs, or demand for an industry or the economy.
  • Not relevant: rallies, endorsements, legal fights, media attacks, cheerleading with no policy or mechanism.
  • Judge the next year, assuming the stated policy happens, using only what was public at the post's time.
  • Neutral = no identifiable material effect. Uncertain = too thin, or effects compete.
  • Steel tariffs: steel positive, autos often negative (they buy steel). Give each industry its own call.
  • Broad economy = across-the-board claims with no industry split.
